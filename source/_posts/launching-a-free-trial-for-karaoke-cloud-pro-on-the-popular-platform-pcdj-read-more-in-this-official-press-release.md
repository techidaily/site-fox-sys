## Launching: A FREE TRIAL for Karaoke Cloud Pro on the Popular Platform PCDJ! Read More in This Official Press Release

[![Karaoke Cloud Pro Press Release - 14-Day Free Trial](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/kcp14daytrialcoverimage-coverimage.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/kcp14daytrialcoverimage-coverimage.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "Karaoke Cloud Pro Press Release 14-day Free Trial")

#### Karaoke Cloud Pro, the world’s first and only legal professional karaoke subscription service for commercial use, can now be tried at no cost for 14 days in Digital 1 Audio’s popular PCDJ Karaoki software.

[LEARN MORE ABOUT KARAOKE CLOUD PRO ![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/karaoke-cloud-pro-logo.png?fit=250%2C74&ssl=1 "karaoke-cloud-pro-logo")](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

CLEARWATER, FL JULY 05, 2016

Digital 1 Audio, Inc ([http://www.pcdj.com](http://www.prweb.net/Redirect.aspx?id=aHR0cDovL3d3dy5wY2RqLmNvbQ==)), creators of the leading [**karaoke software**](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) for professional karaoke hosts and karaoke venues, in coordination with Karaoke Cloud Pro providers Digitrax Entertainment, now allow the karaoke subscription service to be available on a trial basis, free of charge for 14 days in [PCDJ Karaoki](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/). The cost for subscribers that continue service after the trial period will pay $49.99-a-month.

_“Staying commensurate with pricing for music videos, professional audio subscriptions, and many SaaS offerings, we feel the price of $49.99 is a great value, and will enable karaoke jockeys to enjoy the legal music offered with the Karaoke Cloud,” said Joe Vangieri, CEO of DigiTrax. “As an additional incentive, we are including over 7,000 of our world-class backing tracks. Who at your gig does not have an Android or iPhone with which they can instantly look up lyrics via Google, Soundhound, or LyricFind?”_

[LEARN MORE ABOUT KARAOKI (KARAOKE SOFTWARE) ![Karaoke Software at Club](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/karaoki-withcloud-in-club.jpg?fit=300%2C225&ssl=1 "Karaoki Karaoke Software At Club")](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

The no-commitment 14-day complimentary trial will allow KJs and venues that host karaoke nights to use all features with no limitation of the professional online/offline karaoke music subscription service. Karaoke Cloud Pro files are retrieved for offline use and work just like standard high-quality karaoke files in PCDJ Karaoki. For those that do not already own Karaoki, a 14-day no-limitation trial of the karaoke software can also be downloaded from the PCDJ website.

Here are a few additional notes on the Karaoke Cloud Pro karaoke subscription service:

* High-quality karaoke recordings in all genres, with new songs added regularly at no additional cost.
* World’s first and only legal professional karaoke subscription for commercial use.
* Subscribers receive certification that can be printed out and kept on their person on in the performance venue.
* Karaoke tracks are downloaded to your local or external hard drive so they can be used offline (30 days at a time).
* Use Karaoke Cloud Pro tracks with other optional services baked into Karaoki such as the SongbookDB plugin.
* Print out or export the Karaoke Cloud Pro song list from Karaoki to create a song book.
* The service is directly integrated into Karaoki, so no need to use a third-party website to view and download karaoke music.

PCDJ Karaoki’s retail price is $99, but can be purchased for only $79 for new Karaoke Cloud Pro subscribers. Karaoke Cloud Pro is complimentary for 14 days and $49.99 a month thereafter. Subscribers can cancel anytime without penalty.
