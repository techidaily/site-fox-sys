##  Karaoke software creators, PCDJ, announced a much anticipated update to PCDJ Karaoki, designed to significantly improve karaoke event production by making karaoke show management and singer rotation more robust than ever before.

 Clearwater, Florida, USA (PRWEB) February 13, 2014

**PCDJ Karaoki's new, highly intuitive singers rotation list, a new CDG engine that’s 3X more efficient, innovative CDG transparency mode, and more, make this already phenomenal software even better.** 

 "PCDJ has created professional **karaoke software** products for over a decade now,” says General Manager Ryan Sherr. "One thing that KJ’s can always use more help with is managing their singers and rotation throughout their show.” Sherr continues, “Much of what’s new in our latest version of Karaoki was crowd sourced. Our users wanted a _**better singer rotation**_ and **_singer history_**, and I think we’ve delivered. In addition, the _**CDG engine is more robust and 3X more efficient**_ so the program is more responsive and snappy.”  

**Here are some of the notable new features and/or improvements in PCDJ Karaoki:** 

* Significantly Enhanced Singer Rotation List and Singer Management
* New CDG playback engine that's 3X more efficient than in previous releases
* “Transparency Mode” that allows you to put images behind your CDG Lyrics (slide show)
* New “Fade To Stop” option on the Karaoke Player
* Improved Karaoke Cloud Pro Subscription Support
* Many “under the hood” performance enhancements

 PCDJ Karaoki manufacturer’s suggested retail price is $99 (Existing PCDJ software users can call for special upgrade pricing).
